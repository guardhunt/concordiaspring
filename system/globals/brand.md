# Brand — ConcordiaSpring

> **Source:** `PROJECT.md` section 2
> **Project:** Yoga & somatics school, Asheville, NC

---

## Voice

Warm, embodied, philosophical. Poetic without being obscure. Not clinical, not corporate, not "wellness industry generic." Language draws from somatic experience, nature, and relational presence.

---

## Visual Aesthetic

- **Palette:** Warm earth tones — terracotta, clay, warm taupe, muted sage green
- **Feel:** Soft, not stark. Low contrast where possible. Handmade paper texture.
- **Typography:** Cormorant Garamond (headings) + DM Sans (body)
- **Spacing:** Generous whitespace. The layout should breathe.
- **Imagery:** Nature-rooted. No stock-photo yoga poses.
- **Dark mode:** Optional and secondary — light mode is the primary experience.

---

## Design Principles

1. **Warm over clinical** — earthy tones, soft edges, no harsh contrasts
2. **Embodied over abstract** — language and visuals rooted in felt experience
3. **Minimal over busy** — clean navigation, no heavy shadows or borders
4. **Nature over performance** — imagery and copy avoid fitness-industry clichés
5. **Breathing room** — whitespace is a design element, not empty space
